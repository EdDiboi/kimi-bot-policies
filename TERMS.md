# Kimi Discord Bot Terms of Use

Updated October 26, 2026.

Kimi is a Discord bot that answers questions and joins conversations using Moonshot AI. This edition runs AI and support features in one configured server. Use of the bot must follow [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms), [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines), and these terms. You must meet Discord's minimum age requirement and any higher local requirement.

## Using Kimi

Use the bot lawfully and respect other people. Do not use it for harassment, exploitation, scams, credential theft, or illegal content. Do not submit passwords, access tokens, confidential material, or sensitive personal information. Share only material you are entitled to send to the bot and its services.

AI answers can be wrong or incomplete. Check important information independently. Kimi is not a substitute for professional advice or emergency services, and its persona does not give it authority to make promises about another company's accounts or products. Features may change or become unavailable.

## Privacy and your choices

Read the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) before sending content. It explains the AI requests, temporary channel context, encrypted settings, private tickets, and deletion limits.

Run `/privacy optout` to stop processing and restrict posting in the AI channel. Run `/privacy optin` or use **Opt Back In** to restore the changes Kimi made. Other moderation rules still apply. Administrators can bypass Discord's posting restrictions but are still excluded from AI processing while opted out.

`/privacy delete` clears temporary AI context, deletes your tracked support tickets, and opts you out. Necessary refusal and access-restoration records remain until they can be removed safely. The command does not delete ordinary Discord posts or recall information already sent to an external provider.

## Help and support

Run `/help`, press **▶** twice, and select **Open a ticket** to report an issue or make a data request. Tickets are private Discord threads for the requester, invited operators, and staff whose Discord permissions allow access. Their contents are never sent to the AI or saved as a local transcript.

Closing a ticket keeps it archived. Use `/privacy tickets` in the support channel for Reopen or Delete controls. The Delete button requires confirmation. Tickets are scheduled for deletion 30 days after creation, subject to bot availability and the permissions needed to delete them.

Operators review reports and may limit or disable the bot to address abuse or technical problems. Server administrators remain responsible for their community rules and can remove the bot or change its channel access. The published policy and terms will be updated when the service changes.
